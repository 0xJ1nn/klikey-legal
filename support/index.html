KliKey is a keyboard with themes, sounds and haptics. If it isn’t typing the way it should, the answer is almost always in the first two sections below. Anything else: jinultimate1995@gmail.com — a real person reads it.

Contact. Email jinultimate1995@gmail.com with your phone model, iOS or Android version, and what happened. We usually answer within two working days. There is no chat, no forum and no account to sign in to.

Turn on KliKey · iPhone and iPad

  1. Open Settings → General → Keyboard → Keyboards → Add New Keyboard… and pick KliKey.
  2. Tap KliKey in that list, then turn on Allow Full Access. Sound, haptics, themes and the clipboard panel need it; typing works without it. KliKey asks for it only to play sound and to share settings with the app.
  3. In any app, tap a text field, then press and hold the 🌐 globe key and choose KliKey.

If KliKey doesn’t appear on the globe key, check step 1 again: the keyboard has to be in the list before iOS will offer it.

Turn on KliKey · Android

  1. Open the KliKey app and tap Set as default, or go to Settings → System → Keyboard → On-screen keyboard → Manage keyboards and turn KliKey on.
  2. Pick KliKey as your input method. On Samsung phones, also turn off Settings → General management → Keyboard list and default → Show keyboard button if the switcher keeps taking over.

Common questions

The keyboard isn’t showing, or it went back to the Apple keyboard

iOS sometimes switches keyboards on its own in a field it considers sensitive. KliKey cannot appear in password, PIN, one-time-code or card-number fields: iOS does not allow third-party keyboards there, and no keyboard app can change that. Outside those fields, press and hold 🌐 and pick KliKey again. If it disappears mid-sentence, update to the latest version — the current build keeps the keyboard’s memory well under the limit where iOS would shut it down.

No sound or no haptics

Both need Allow Full Access on iOS (Settings → General → Keyboard → Keyboards → KliKey). Then check the silent switch, the volume, and KliKey → Sound in the app. Ninja mode is silent by design.

No suggestions, or the wrong language

Add the language in KliKey → Languages; the language key on the keyboard switches between the ones you added. Leaf languages such as Thai, Lao and Khmer read words from their own dictionary instead of a word list.

I bought Pro but the themes are still locked

Open KliKey → Pro and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account you bought with. Purchases made on iPhone are handled by Apple and do not appear on Android (and the other way round); the one-time lifetime purchase is per store. If restore still doesn’t work, email us the receipt from Apple or Google.

Refunds

Apple handles App Store refunds (reportaproblem.apple.com) and Google handles Play refunds (play.google.com). We cannot issue or reverse a store charge ourselves.

Cancel a subscription

iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Android: Play Store → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

Ninja and Incognito

Ninja hides the keys (blank, silent, a panic swipe wipes the draft). Incognito stops KliKey remembering anything (no learned words, no clipboard or emoji history). They are free, they work together, and they never turn on by themselves except Incognito in password, PIN, card and one-time-code fields.

Where my words and settings live

On your phone. Learned words can sync to a hidden folder in your own Google Drive if you turn sync on; we run no server and never receive them. Everything is deleted by Settings → Erase learned words or by removing the app. Details in the Privacy Policy.

A theme’s keys look different from the preview

Theme cards draw the keyboard at a fixed size; on a very small or very wide screen the keys scale a little differently. If something looks broken rather than scaled, send a screenshot — that is a bug and we want it.
