Privacy Policy

Last updated 15 October 2026

KliKey is a keyboard app for Android and iOS. This policy explains what KliKey does with your information — and the short version is simple: everything stays on your phone, unless you turn on sync, which keeps your learned words in your own Google Drive.

The short version

KliKey works on your phone. The keyboard has no network access and sends nothing anywhere. What you type is never stored on a server, because there is no server.

What the keyboard can see

Like any keyboard, KliKey sees the text in the field you are typing into so it can show suggestions. That text is used on the device only, to type and to predict, and is discarded as you go.

What is stored

If learning is on, KliKey keeps a small list of words and word pairs you actually use, on your device (and in your own Google Drive only if you turn on sync), so typing gets better. It excludes passwords, number pads, e-mail and URL fields, and any app that asks for no suggestions.

Learned words & sync (optional, free, off by default)

Sync is off unless you turn it on, and it covers learned words only — never what you type. When you choose Google Drive and tap Connect Google Drive, Google asks you to let KliKey use its own hidden folder in your Drive, and a copy of your learned words is kept there so they come back on another phone connected to the same Drive. The folder is invisible in the Drive interface and other apps cannot open it; Google, which hosts it, can. The copy is encrypted, but its key is kept in the same folder, so what protects it is your Google account, not the encryption. KliKey runs no server and never receives it. Disconnecting Drive keeps your words on the phone, and Erase learned words also deletes the Drive copy. You can revoke KliKey's access any time in your Google Account under Data & privacy → Third-party apps.

Ninja and Incognito

Ninja hides what’s on screen: blank keys, no previews, silent, and a panic swipe that wipes the draft. Incognito forgets what you typed: no learned words, no clipboard or emoji history. Use either or both. Incognito can turn on by itself in password, PIN, card, one-time-code and private fields; Ninja only ever turns on when you turn it on.

What we collect

Nothing reaches us. If you turn on sync, your learned words go to your own Google Drive, never to us. There is no analytics, no advertising ID and no account needed to type. Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play or the App Store, which may share a receipt with us to unlock Pro.

Clipboard and recents

If you copy text, KliKey may offer it in its clipboard panel on this device only. Incognito pauses it, and you can clear it any time in Settings.

Your control

Settings → Erase learned words deletes everything KliKey has learned, permanently, including the synced copy. Turning the keyboard off or uninstalling it removes the rest.

Children

KliKey is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes we will show the new version in the app and update the date above.
